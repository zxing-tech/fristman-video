## Notes on selection

- All 11 photos were screened at full resolution for legible brand names,
  logos, watermarks, and unsafe work practices before being picked; several
  strong candidates were rejected for carrying a visible company or product
  name (e.g. a "Long An International" crane, a "TOTAL" drum, a "CAT"
  excavator badge, an "ISIS" hi-vis vest, a "Liebherr" crane jib, a named
  vessel hull) and are not used anywhere in this set.
- `facility-refinery.jpg`, `progress-phase-02-foundation.jpg`,
  `progress-phase-04-loadout.jpg` and `photo-structural-phase.jpg` are
  center-cropped from the originals; `photo-structural-phase.jpg` and
  `progress-phase-04-loadout.jpg` are additionally cropped to exclude a
  branded shipping container and a crane-jib logo respectively that sat in
  the original frame.
- `progress-capture-approach.jpg`'s source photo is titled "closed market
  hall" by the photographer; it is used here purely for its visual
  resemblance to a dim industrial corridor, not as a real plant photo — the
  description above states only what is visible.
- `facility-fabrication-yard.jpg` is the weakest fit against the original
  brief (a close-up pipe stack rather than a wide/elevated yard view) — see
  the delivery report for details.
- `facility-offshore-module.jpg` and `photo-team-portrait.jpg` were replaced
  after launch: the originals (a hazy, distant sunset platform and a posed
  daylight two-person portrait) read too weakly at their card sizes. Both are
  center-cropped from their Pexels originals (offshore module to 4:3,
  team portrait to 3:4) with no branded content in either crop.
